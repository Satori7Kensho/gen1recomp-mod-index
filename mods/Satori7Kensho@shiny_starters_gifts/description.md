**Shiny Starters, Gifts & NPC Trades** lets you receive shiny starters, shiny story gift Pokémon, and shiny Pokémon from in-game NPC trades in Gen1Recomp.

### What’s New in v1.3.0
- **FireRed & LeafGreen support** added
- New independent option for **shiny in-game NPC trades**
- 
### Changes in v1.3.1
- Updated display name to “Shiny Starters, Gifts & NPC Trades”
- Minor cosmetic and documentation polish
- No functional changes

### Options (all independent, default ON)
- **SHINY STARTERS** – Makes the starter from Oak’s or Elm’s lab shiny
- **SHINY GIFTS** – Makes other scripted story gifts and gift eggs shiny (Yellow’s later Bulbasaur/Charmander/Squirtle gifts use this)
- **SHINY TRADES** – Makes Pokémon received from supported in-game NPC trades shiny

Each option only affects its own category. You can turn them on or off independently.

### Important
- Enable the mod **and** set your options **before** receiving the Pokémon
- Fully close and reopen Gen1Recomp after enabling the mod or changing options

### Supported Games
- Red, Blue, Yellow (Gen 1)
- Gold, Silver, Crystal (Gen 2)
- **FireRed & LeafGreen (Gen 3)** ← new in v1.3.0

Requires Gen1Recomp **v0.3.5 or later**.

The mod only affects newly received starters, scripted gifts, and NPC trades. It does **not** change wild encounters, trainer Pokémon, or link/player trades.
